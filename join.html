The arrangement

Simple on purpose.

The economics are stated up front. Nothing here depends on a quota, a ramp, or a franchise layer.

01

95/5 from day one

Not after a production threshold. From your first transaction, the split is 95% to you and 5% to the brokerage.

02

No mandatory monthly fees

There is no bill just for having a desk here. You are not paying dues while you figure out the next quarter.

03

Pay only for what you use

Services are optional. If you want something, you can pay for it. If you don’t, you don’t.

04

Your name on the work

Freedom to build your own brand. Western Reserve is the brokerage. It is not a costume you have to wear.
